# Project Write-Up: Heart Disease Analysis Dashboard

## Overview

This project aims to develop a comprehensive dashboard using Power BI to analyze and visualize key performance indicators (KPIs) related to patient outcomes. The focus will be on survival rates, age-related patterns, and risk factors impacting health. 

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Survival Rate:** The percentage of patients who survive over a specified period.
- **Average Age of Survival:** The mean age of patients who have survived.
- **Total Survival:** The total number of patients who survived.
- **Total Death:** The total number of patients who did not survive.

These metrics provide a foundational understanding of patient health outcomes and can help inform medical decisions and healthcare policies.

## Data Visualization Components

### 1. Survival Count and Average Serum Creatinine by Age Group

- **Purpose:** This chart highlights how survival rates vary across different age groups and their relationship with serum creatinine levels.
- **Importance:** Identifying trends can help correlate kidney function with survival rates and assist in formulating treatment plans.

### 2. Survival Count and Average Ejection Fraction by Age Group

- **Purpose:** This visualization focuses on heart function among survivors, showcasing how ejection fraction changes with age.
- **Importance:** Understanding heart performance variations can guide interventions tailored to patient age.

### 3. Survival Rate by Age Group Line Chart

- **Purpose:** This line chart shows how survival rates fluctuate with age, enabling identification of age-related risk patterns.
- **Importance:** It aids in recognizing critical age groups that require targeted healthcare strategies.

### 4. Ribbon Chart for Health Risk Factors

- **Purpose:** This ribbon chart visualizes the impact of smoking, high blood pressure, diabetes, and anemia across different age groups.
- **Importance:** The size of the ribbons represents the contribution of these factors to health risks at various ages, allowing for a clear understanding of how lifestyle and health conditions interact with age.

### 5. Slicer for Data Filtering by Sex

- **Purpose:** A slicer will enable users to filter the dashboard data based on gender.
- **Importance:** This feature allows for a more detailed analysis of survival rates and health trends among different genders.

## Project Coverage

### 1. Data Ingestion

The project begins with the extraction of relevant patient data from healthcare databases. The data will include demographic information, health metrics, and outcomes over time. 

### 2. Summarizing KPI Metrics

Next, the KPIs will be calculated and summarized to provide an overarching view of patient outcomes. This step ensures that the dashboard's indicators are based on accurate and up-to-date data.

### 3. Developing Charts & Visual Elements

We'll create the various charts and visual elements outlined above. Power BI offers robust tools for data visualization, allowing us to depict complex relationships in an understandable way.

### 4. Structuring a Professional Dashboard Layout

The dashboard will be structured to ensure ease of use. Key sections will be logically arranged to guide users through the data insights smoothly.
